# Markets and languages

## Languages

AERUN includes English and French, for both the storefront and the theme editor. To sell in another language:

1. Add the language in *Settings → Languages*.
2. Translate the theme texts and your content with the **Translate & Adapt** app. Metaobject entries and metafields marked as translatable are translated there too.

Turn on the language selector in the Footer section (*Show language selector*).

## Countries and currencies

Set up markets in *Settings → Markets*. The footer's *Show country/region selector* lets shoppers change country and currency. Prices are converted by Shopify.

## Free shipping progress

*Theme settings → Cart → Free shipping thresholds* takes one threshold per currency, for example `EUR:100, CHF:110`. The cart shows how much is left to reach it. Without a threshold for the shopper's currency, nothing is shown.

These amounts are only a display: set the same thresholds in your shipping rates (*Settings → Shipping and delivery*), which decide the real price at checkout.

## Units

Specs keep the unit you enter (grams, millimetres, millilitres…). The theme adds the conversion in brackets, for example `228 g (8.0 oz)`. Turn it off with *Show unit conversions* in *Theme settings → Running data*.

## Prices

The theme shows Shopify's prices, compare-at prices, unit prices (for example per 100 g on nutrition products) and subscription prices from selling plans. Tax display follows your Shopify tax settings.
