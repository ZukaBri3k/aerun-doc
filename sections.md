# Building pages

## Sections, blocks and presets

- A **section** is a full-width part of a page: a hero, a product grid, a FAQ.
- A **block** is a piece inside a section: a heading, a button, a card, a spec table.
- A **preset** is a ready-made version of a section, for example *Grid → Key figures* or *Featured collection → Best sellers*.

In the theme editor, select **Add section** and pick from the list. The full list with every setting is in the [reference]({{ '/reference/' | relative_url }}).

## Main sections

**Hero**: the main message of a page, on an image or a video, overlaid or side by side. Videos never play with sound and can be paused.

**Slideshow**: several campaigns, one at a time. Autoplay is off by default; when on, visitors can pause it.

**Media and text**: an image or a video beside a heading, text and buttons.

**Grid**: cards, key figures, quotes, logos or portraits in columns. Its presets cover benefits, key figures, steps, testimonials, press, awards, commitments, people, gallery and community.

**Banner**: a campaign or a call to action. Its countdown needs a real end date with a time and a time zone offset, for example `2026-12-01T09:00+01:00`. When the date passes, the banner shows a text you choose or disappears. Never use a countdown that restarts or a date that isn't real.

**FAQ**: questions and answers as accordions, from your FAQ entries (*Content → Metaobjects*). Filter by category or by handles, so one answer written once is reused everywhere.

**Featured collection**: products from a collection, as a grid or a carousel. The *Best sellers* preset works best with the collection sorted by best selling in the admin.

**Collection tabs**: up to five collections under tabs. The other tabs load when opened, which keeps the page fast.

**Featured product**: one product with its images, variants and buy buttons, for a launch page.

**Featured articles**: the latest articles of a blog, or articles you pick.

**Kit**: products worn or used together, each with its own options. *Add the selection* adds each product at its own price; it is not a bundle discount.

**Checklist**: a race-day or packing list visitors can tick. Nothing is saved.

**Recently viewed**: the last products seen on this device, with a button to clear them.

**Testimonials**: quotes you selected, with who said them and where.

**Stores** and **Events**: your points of sale and events, from their metaobject entries.

**Contact form**: Shopify's contact form, sent to your store email, with extra fields if you need them.

**Reviews**: an area for your reviews app's block.

**Custom Liquid**: your own Liquid or HTML, for advanced cases.

## Good practice

- Give each long page an *Anchored sub-navigation* section and an *Anchor label* on its main sections.
- Use real content: real dates, real stock, real figures with their source. The theme has no fake urgency on purpose.
- Write alternative text for your images in the admin.
