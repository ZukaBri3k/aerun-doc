# Navigation

## Header

Create menus in *Online Store → Navigation*, then set them in the Header section:

- **Menu**: the main menu. Nested menu items become dropdowns.
- **Mega menu**: add a *Mega menu feature* block to show an image and a link inside the dropdown of a menu item.
- **Customer account menu**: links shown to signed-in customers.
- **Search**: predictive search shows products, collections, articles and pages while typing. Turn it off with *Show suggestions while typing*. *Popular searches menu* sets suggestions shown before typing.
- **Sticky header** and **Color scheme**.
- **Transparent on home page**: the header sits over the first section of the home page. Choose a light or dark *Transparent header text* to match the image.

## Announcement bar

Add one or more *Announcement* blocks. With several, *Rotate announcements* shows them one after the other, and visitors can pause the rotation. *Let visitors dismiss the bar* adds a close button.

## Footer

The Footer section takes blocks:

- **Brand**: logo and a short text.
- **Menu**: a menu from *Online Store → Navigation*.
- **Newsletter**: a signup form; subscribers go to your Shopify customers.
- **Contact**: the customer service details of the theme settings.
- **Text**: free text.

The section settings show social media links (set in *Theme settings → Social media*), the country/region and language selectors, policy links and payment icons.

## Anchor navigation on long pages

On a long landing or product page, add the *Anchored sub-navigation* section. Then give an *Anchor label* to each section that should appear in it. The bar lists those sections in page order and stays visible while scrolling.
